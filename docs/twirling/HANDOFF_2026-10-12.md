## Where we are

Goal: gliding and twirling at low [ATP] on the explicit-S2 single-head model, for comparison with Beausang 2008.
Biology first, no tuning to assays. jba prefers long runs and seconds-scale observables.

1. **The model glides at saturating ATP.** 2 mM, d400, rupture off, one lawn, 0.3 s: 7.4 ± 0.5 µm/s, ~3 heads bound.
2. **At low ATP it creeps rather than glides.** At 10 µM with k_ATP = 2.4 /µM/s, every long rupture-off run slows after
   0.1–0.3 s to 0.03–0.09 µm/s. Hinge stiffness, filament flexibility, an S2-direction gate, S2 length and density did
   not change that on one lawn.
3. **Cause:** "anchor" heads. Forward-stroking heads whose S2 points toward the pointed end average −0.3 to −0.5 pN over
   an attachment; the rest average +0.1 to +0.2 pN. Rigor takes 92–97 % of bound time. It is many weak pulls, not a wall.
4. **k_ATP = 5 /µM/s at 20 µM (jba's latest choice) gives ~0.14 µm/s** on four lawns over 1 s, the bottom of Beausang's
   0.1–0.5 µm/s range and about a fifth of the detachment-limited ideal (0.7 µm/s).
5. **Twirl is not resolved.** Net roll is positive on all four lawns (+0.4 to +2.1 turns over ~0.13 µm), pooled drift
   1.6 ± 0.8 turns/s, against roll diffusion of ~0.73 turns²/s. Beausang: pitch 0.47 ± 0.2 µm (~2 turns/µm), ~20 % of
   filaments twirl. If our drift is real it is ~5× too tight.

## Rules and traps

- GPU work only through `run_gpu_monitored.sh`, recorder checked first. Never edit BoA-v1ref. `sim_server.py` and the
  viewer stay verbatim. Commit and push only when jba asks.
- **At most three long runs at once.** Each JVM grows to ~8 GB (`-Xmx12G`); four exhausted the 31 GB host and the kernel
  killed one. Concurrency does not add throughput anyway: 1 run ~250 steps/s, 4 runs ~90 each, 7 runs ~50 each.
- Launch detached (`setsid nohup … &`); background tasks tied to the session are killed at ~30 minutes. In tmux this
  still applies to anything Claude launches.
- Do not `pkill -f` by script name; it kills the invoking shell. Kill by PID.
- One lawn is a pilot, not a result (the existence gate in `CLAUDE.md`). Report resolution before effect size.
- Net roll over time is mostly diffusion. Report drift and diffusion separately.
- No speed tuning. Twirl handedness (ε sign) is an input, not a target.
