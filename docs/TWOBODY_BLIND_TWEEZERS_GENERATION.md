## 1. What was generated

A Langevin dual-trap actin-dumbbell (filament between two endpoint traps, filament Brownian ON) driven by
**naturally captured** two-body motors (the validated 3E search) undergoing the 3F sequence:
Brownian search → stereospecific ADP·Pi capture → passive pre-stroke relaxation → Pi release → θ_s: −30° → +30° →
stable ADP dwell.

| item | count |
|---|---|
| stroke events (raw_traces) | **165** (55 per trap stiffness A/B/C) |
| bound-no-release traces (attach, no stroke; unlabeled in raw_traces) | 15 |
| controls | 21 — 9 `no_motor`, 6 `motor_present`, 6 `no_motor_perturbation_calibration` |
| calibration traces (long, no-motor) | 3 (one per stiffness) |
| datasets | 2 — `ideal_observable_only` (50 kHz, noise-free) & `instrument_realistic` (20 kHz, filtered, noisy, drifted) |
| total blind files | 413 (verified intact) |

- **Trap stiffness:** 0.02, 0.05, 0.10 pN/nm per trap (levels A/B/C).
- **Preload:** {−1.0, −0.5, 0.0, +0.5, +1.0} pN — assisting / near-zero / opposing (a visible trap-center offset).
- **Perturbations (both sides of the stroke):** during the relaxed pre-stroke dwell AND the stable post-stroke
  dwell, both trap centers are stepped by known amounts — square pulses at amplitudes 1/2/3 nm (~3 ms half-period,
  slow) then a fast ±2 nm burst (~0.6 ms). Bead response ≤ ~0.5 nm ⇒ approximately linear; they do not trigger the
  (time-imposed) chemical transition.
- **Instrument-realistic model:** 20 kHz sampling, 1st-order 3 kHz low-pass, 0.8 nm detector noise, ~2.5 nm slow
  common-mode drift, ±10 % trap-calibration uncertainty. Paired to its ideal twin by trace ID.

## 2. Frozen private-truth definitions (EXTERNAL measurements, not assigned constants)

All truth is computed on the noise-free deterministic dumbbell by the SAME external protocol an experimentalist
would use (see `PREREGISTRATION.md §1`): **event step** (pre→post plateau of the bead COM), **unloaded step**
(zero-preload intercept; also the compliance-free zero-trap-stiffness intercept), **apparent stiffness** (raw
follow fraction), **crossbridge stiffness** `k_xb = 2·k_ax·(Δ−δ)/δ` (compliance-corrected), measured **pre-stroke**
(ADP·Pi dwell) AND **post-stroke** (ADP dwell). Representative truth (private): k_xb_pre ≈ 0.64, k_xb_post ≈ 0.62
pN/nm (a small, real pre>post difference); per-stiffness zero-preload step 7.5/6.9/6.0 nm at 0.02/0.05/0.10;
compliance-free intrinsic step ≈ 7.9 nm; the assigned F8 spring (1.0 pN/nm) is **not** the stiffness truth.

## 3. Assay physics (generator-side validation)

Calibration traces recover k by equipartition; attachment shows a clear variance drop; a pointed-first working step
scales with trap stiffness (the compliance division of an intrinsic ~8 nm stroke); the deterministic k_xb is a
clean linear-elastic response. The step is weakly load-dependent over ±1 pN (~1 %/pN) and strongly
stiffness-dependent — an honest, non-inflated property. Whether the analyst can recover step and (pre/post)
stiffness from the thermal + instrument noise is the identifiability question the challenge poses; it is NOT
pre-judged here.

## 4. Blinding & integrity (validated before sealing)

- **Allowed exports only:** time, bead positions, trap-center commands, calibrated stiffnesses (by level),
  perturbation commands, sampling/filter metadata, calibration records, no-motor controls, barbed/pointed polarity
  marker. **Forbidden fields audited absent:** binding/Pi-release timestamps, chemical-state/event-selection
  labels, converter/head/lever coordinates, F8 extension, internal forces/energies, assigned spring constants,
  expected step/stiffness — none appear in any trace/index/metadata file; control labels are experimentally honest
  (`no_motor`/`motor_present`/`no_motor_perturbation_calibration`); filenames are randomized `tr_<hash>`; no
  private-truth references or repository paths in the package.
- **Package validation:** every event from natural stereospecific capture; integrated Pi-stroke polarity-correct
  (165/165 pointed-first); pre-transition axial trap force ~0 at zero preload; perturbations in the linear regime;
  calibration + controls sufficient; **restart reproducible (two independent generations bit-identical)**;
  copyable + analyzable without repository access.
- **Hashes:** `blind_package/MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` (412 files, verifies with `sha256sum -c`); a copy + a private
  manifest are in `private_truth/`.
